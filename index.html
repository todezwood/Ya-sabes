Where to keep going, in the neighborhood

You don't need to do this alone, and you don't need to pay for it. Places in and around the Excelsior that run free classes, computer access, and job help:

  • Excelsior Branch, San Francisco Public Library — free computer and internet access, and free digital-skills classes
  • SFPL system-wide — free LinkedIn Learning and tech-help appointments with a library card
  • Mission Neighborhood Centers — workforce and family services, bilingual
  • Excelsior Action Group — small-business support along Mission Street
  • SF Office of Economic & Workforce Development (OEWD) — free job training and placement programs

Check current hours, languages and eligibility directly with each organization — this page lists names only, not schedules. Nothing here is legal, medical, or financial advice.
